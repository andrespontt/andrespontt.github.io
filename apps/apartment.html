🌧️ APARTMENT

Walk your apartment on a stormy night — living room, bedroom, kitchen — then head down through the lobby via the elevator or the stairs to the rainy street outside.

Desktop: click to look around, WASD to move, E to interact.
Mobile: drag left to move, drag right to look, tap the button to interact.
